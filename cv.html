CV

I am a Ph.D. researcher studying small-molecule drug delivery and nanomedicine for neuroinflammation through multiscale molecular simulation. By integrating drug delivery experiments, all-atom molecular dynamics, and Martini 3 coarse-grained simulations, I investigate a continuum from purinergic drug discovery to LNP structure, drug loading, and membrane interactions. My long-term goal is to develop simulation-guided design strategies for CNS- and blood–brain barrier (BBB)-targeted nanomedicine.
