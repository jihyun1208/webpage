My research focuses on molecular dynamics (MD) simulations, lipid nanoparticle (LNP)-based drug delivery, and purinergic therapeutics targeting neuroinflammation. My goal is to translate molecular-level understanding into meaningful therapeutic strategies.

The question that drives my research is simple: “How can we translate what happens at the molecular level into therapies that truly benefit patients?”

I believe effective nanomedicines are not developed by chance. They can be designed more rationally when we understand how lipid composition, drug molecules, and the physical properties of membranes interact with one another.

Currently, I use Martini coarse-grained molecular dynamics simulations to investigate how LNP composition influences structural organization, small-molecule loading and distribution, membrane behavior, and drug–lipid interactions. Through simulation-guided formulation design, I aim to reduce experimental trial and error and develop more efficient drug delivery systems.

Three principles guide my research:

  • Translate molecular insights into therapeutic impact
  • Ground my research in data and evidence
  • Keep people at the heart of scientific research

Looking ahead, I aim to advance simulation-guided nanomedicine design by bridging molecular modeling with clinical insight.
