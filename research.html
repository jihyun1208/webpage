Molecular Dynamics Simulation

Martini coarse-grained molecular dynamics makes microsecond-scale lipid assembly behavior computationally tractable. I use it to quantify how molecules arrange themselves and move inside lipid bilayers and nanoparticles.

Lipid Nanoparticle Drug Delivery

Lipid nanoparticles are the delivery vehicle behind mRNA vaccines and much of modern nanomedicine. Yet which lipid composition loads which drug, and how it releases, is still largely explored empirically. I work on mapping composition–structure–behavior relationships in simulation so that formulation design becomes predictable.

Purinergic Therapeutics & Translation

Purinergic signaling is involved in inflammation, pain, and tissue regeneration. I am interested in translational work that carries molecular modeling results toward real therapeutic strategies, connecting computation, experiment, and clinical context.
